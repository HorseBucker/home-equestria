Tool

Options

Selected

Nothing selected.

Layers

Official map (tracing)

Or hold Alt: drag moves the map, wheel resizes it, Shift+wheel turns it.

Keys

Drag with the right or middle button (or hold Space) to pan; wheel zooms.
Placing a house or landmark: press and drag toward where it should face. To turn one later, select it and drag its door dot (house) or arrow (landmark).
Q/E turn a house, landmark or note 5 degrees (Shift: 45).
A note can face a way too: press and drag when placing it, or select it and drag its arrow (or set Facing; empty = none).
Enter or double-click finishes a road, river or lake; Esc cancels.
T flips a selected house between real home and background.
Del removes the selection. Ctrl+Z undoes. Ctrl+D copies a house.
